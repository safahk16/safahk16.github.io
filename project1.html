PROJECT 01 · DIGITAL FORENSICS

Digital Forensic Investigation

A digital forensic investigation into suspected criminal activity involving the National Art Gallery.

Autopsy E01 MD5 Evidence Analysis

OVERVIEW

The Investigation

This university project involved investigating a forensic disk image for evidence relating to a suspected organised attack on the National Art Gallery.

The investigation focused on identifying and analysing digital evidence including emails, documents, web history, deleted data and other digital artefacts.

TOOLS & TECHNOLOGY

What I Used

Autopsy

Used to examine the forensic disk image, search for artefacts and analyse evidence.

E01 Forensic Image

The investigation was performed using a forensic disk image rather than directly accessing the original device.

MD5 Hashing

Used to verify the integrity of the forensic image before and after analysis.

Strings

Used to investigate the contents of an audio file and identify a hidden message.

METHODOLOGY

How I Investigated the Evidence

01

Preserved Evidence Integrity

The forensic image was analysed in a read-only environment and its MD5 hash was verified.

02

Analysed the Disk Image

Autopsy was used to examine the forensic image and identify relevant digital artefacts.

03

Searched for Evidence

Keyword searches, metadata analysis, deleted data recovery and timeline analysis were used to investigate the case.

04

Cross-Referenced Findings

Emails, documents, searches and other artefacts were compared to build a timeline of relevant events.

KEY FINDINGS

What I Discovered

Hidden Communication

An audio file named Crazydave.mp3 contained a hidden spoken message. Analysis using the strings command helped identify the relevant section of the file.

Internal Information

Evidence included emails and documents relating to confidential gallery information, including insurance documents and security schedules.

Deleted Evidence

A deleted message was recovered which contained threats directed towards another individual and referred to a planned gallery heist.

INVESTIGATION TIMELINE

Key Events

19 June 2012

An email containing the Crazydave.mp3 audio file was sent between the suspects.

28 June 2012

The audio file was accessed and contained hidden instructions relating to VirtualBox.

5 July 2012

Searches relating to rare stamps and their value were identified.

9 July 2012

A deleted threatening email relating to the planned gallery heist was recovered.

10 July 2012

A security rotation document containing guard shift information was identified.

REFLECTION

What I Learned

This project gave me practical experience working with forensic disk images and analysing different types of digital artefacts.

I learned how important evidence integrity, hashing, documentation and a structured investigation process are when analysing digital evidence.

I also developed experience connecting individual artefacts together to build a timeline and understand the wider context of an investigation.
